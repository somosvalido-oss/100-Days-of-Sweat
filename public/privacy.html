Privacy & Terms

This is a personal, non-commercial project that tracks one person's 100-day fitness challenge.

What is collected

Only the site owner's own data: workouts they log, photos they upload, and statistics (views, likes, follower counts) of their own Instagram and TikTok posts, read through the official APIs with their permission.

How it is used

To display progress on this dashboard. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties. Data is stored on Cloudflare (D1 and R2).

Removal

The owner can disconnect any account at any time in the platform's settings and delete all stored data.

Terms

The site is provided as is, for personal use. No other users can sign in.
